# VibeBook design guide for generated games

The game template draws everything: you only choose its ingredients. Make the choices add up to one world that the post's viewer recognises immediately.

- **Light:** pick `timeOfDay` from the photo's real light. Golden or pink sky → sunset. Dark sky or city lights → night. Coloured artificial light → neon. Otherwise → day.
- **Cast:** pick `player`, `obstacles` and `collectible` from the photo's own world. The player is how someone would move through this place. Obstacles are what is really in the way there. Never mix worlds: no sharks on a mountain, no cactus at the beach, unless the post says so.
- **Pace:** match `speed` and `density` to the mood. A calm scenic photo plays chill or normal with low or medium density. Action, sport or a crowd plays fast with high density. Use insane only when the post is about speed.
- **Title:** 2–3 words, specific to the post. Use the place, the thing or the moment ("Bua Tong Ridge Run", "Azimut Bay Sprint"). Avoid filler words like Dash, Rush, Quest, Adventure and Epic unless one really fits.
- **Tagline:** one concrete picture of what the player does, in plain words, max 90 characters. No hype words (epic, ultimate, insane, legendary) and no exclamation marks.
- **Goal:** choose a goal type the player understands from the title. Distance for journeys, score for collecting, survive for staying in a dangerous place.
